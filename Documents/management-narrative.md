# Management Narrative

**FinSight Distribution & Consumer Products Ltd.**
FY2025 compared with FY2024

## Headline

Revenue rose 52.6%, from ₦1.41bn to ₦2.15bn, but costs rose faster, so the loss widened. Total expenses grew 68.1% to ₦3.54bn, and the net loss before tax doubled from ₦691m to ₦1.38bn. Revenue growth did not reach profit because the cost of powering and moving the business grew far faster than sales.

## Key findings

- **Margins fell at every level.** Gross margin dropped from 16.7% to 11.7%, operating margin from -46.3% to -61.8%, and net margin from -49.0% to -63.9%.
- **Product cost is not the main cause.** Cost of goods sold grew 37.6%, slower than revenue. The squeeze comes from the lines around it.
- **Energy, freight and import costs surged.** Diesel and fuel rose 183%, inbound freight 185%, outbound freight 190% and importation and customs 135%. Together, freight and customs rose from about ₦0.32bn to ₦0.83bn.
- **Operating expenses outpaced sales.** They rose 78%, from ₦0.89bn to ₦1.58bn, about 25 points faster than revenue. The facility operations and logistics cost centres each grew from about ₦0.18bn to roughly ₦0.48bn.
- **Seven of eight branches lost more.** Lagos Mainland had the largest swing, from a ₦132m loss to ₦378m. Abuja Central was the only branch that improved.
- **Revenue is uneven.** Monthly revenue moved between -34% and +63% during 2025, which makes cash planning harder.

## Recommendations

1. **Cut energy costs.** Diesel and fuel cost about ₦0.50bn in FY2025. Buy fuel in bulk, service generators on a schedule, and add solar or hybrid power at the highest-use branches. Set a target for diesel cost to grow no faster than revenue.
2. **Renegotiate freight and import costs.** Tender haulage contracts, consolidate shipments, review clearing agents, and stock regional hubs to shorten outbound deliveries. Had diesel, freight and import costs grown only in line with revenue, FY2025 costs would have been about ₦0.57bn lower. That covers most of the ₦684m increase in the net loss.
3. **Put the loss-making branches on a cost and pricing plan.** Start with Lagos Mainland, Lagos Island, Ibadan and Kano. Set monthly cost budgets, review prices to recover cost increases, and study what Abuja did differently.

## Data note

Eight transaction references repeat but carry different dates and amounts, so they were kept. Transactions with no department or cost centre were labeled "Unassigned" and remain in all totals.
